## What is in the image (platform extraction of the actual file)

- Scene: dusk, high-rise luxury interior; floor-to-ceiling windows onto a
  lit city skyline with a river and distant mountains.
- Person: adult woman, long hair, black leather jacket and pants, standing
  at the centre, one hand on a smart desk — composed, present, in command.
- Holographic interfaces (blue): a world map, a glowing globe, an audio
  waveform panel, data panels — floating in the room.
- Desk: glowing touch surface.
- Furnishings: sofa, round coffee table, gold sphere ornament, plants,
  floor lamp; dark luxe modern materials.
- Text: interface labels are illegible (small, blurred) — stated, not guessed.

## Derived design language (applied this session)

| Element | Derivation | Applied in product |
|---|---|---|
| Palette base | dark luxe interior at dusk | existing dark zinc-950 theme retained |
| Accent primary | holographic blue/cyan interfaces | JARVIS life-sign colour #67e8f9 (world view marker, badges, identity accents) — one unmistakable colour reserved for HER |
| Accent secondary | city lights / gold sphere | warm amber reserved for economy data (transactions, salary events) in dashboard stream + economy card |
| Presence | a composed woman IN the room, not a floating widget | JARVIS is a world resident with a home (Sanctum), a body marker among the population, inspectable like anyone else — never an orb-in-a-corner chatbot |
| Interfaces | panels float around the person; the person is the anchor | views stay restrained; the world and its inhabitants are the product, the UI is chrome |
| Lighting | dusk gradient, glow at interfaces | day/night + weather already drive the 3D scene lighting; emissive materials for life-signs |
| Materials | dark, luxe, restrained | no decorative clutter added; shells are labelled honestly rather than faked |

## What was deliberately NOT done

- No reproduction of the image as a background or texture.
- No invented interface content (the image's labels are illegible — so are ours).
- No "futuristic room" set-dressing in place of real functionality: the
  Sanctum is a real building with real rooms the simulation actually uses.
