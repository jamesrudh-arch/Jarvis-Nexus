4.5 City planning

Capital City

Create these interconnected districts:

1. Northern Park.
2. University District.
3. Residential North.
4. Financial District.
5. Commercial District.
6. Government District.
7. Cultural District.
8. Central Station.
9. Entertainment District.
10. Residential South.
11. Mixed-use District.

Default footprint: approximately 5 km × 7 km.

Core grid blocks: 100 m × 150 m.

Radial avenues converge on the Government District.

Western Industrial City

Include:

* Workforce Residential.
* Light Industry.
* Manufacturing.
* Logistics Hub.
* Heavy Industry.
* Freight Rail.
* Highway.
* Power and water utilities.

Provide industrial safety buffers.

Keep freight traffic away from residential streets.

Eastern Technology City

Include:

* University Campus.
* Research Park.
* Technology Campus.
* Startup District.
* Residential District.
* Public Transport.

Southern Regional City

Include:

* Residential areas.
* Schools.
* Town Centre.
* Regional Hospital.
* Food Processing.
* Rail and Logistics.
* Farmland access roads.

Northern Highland City

Include:

* Mountain slopes.
* Lakeside District.
* Old Town.
* Residential Terraces.
* Resource Depot.
* North Highway.
* Rail Halt.

Terrain and streets follow mountain contours.

Coastal Port City

Include:

* Coastal Residential.
* Waterfront.
* Commercial Centre.
* Port Terminals.
* Logistics District.
* Harbour.
* Shipyards.
* Open Sea.

4.6 Village and district requirements

Every settlement includes:

* Main street.
* Residential area.
* Local market.
* School.
* Healthcare facility.
* Public space.
* Agricultural connection.
* Roads.
* Water supply.
* Power supply.

Every district requires:

* ID.
* Name.
* City.
* Type.
* Polygon.
* Area.
* Zoning.
* Building count.
* Population capacity.
* Road connections.
* Utilities.
* Landmarks.

Road hierarchy:

* Highway.
* Arterial.
* Collector.
* Local.
* Service.
* Pedestrian.

Every road segment stores:

* Road class.
* Lanes.
* Speed limit.
* One-way flag.
* Surface.
* Connected node IDs.

Intersections must support signals and roundabouts.
