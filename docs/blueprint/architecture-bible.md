PART 8 — ARCHITECTURE AND BUILDING SYSTEMS

Create a modular architecture kit.

Buildings must not all look identical.

Required categories:

Residential

* Detached houses.
* Semi-detached houses.
* Terraced houses.
* Bungalows.
* Duplexes.
* Rural houses.
* Apartments.
* High-rises.
* Luxury apartments.
* Affordable housing.
* Social housing.
* Mansions.
* Mixed-use residential.

Commercial

* Shops.
* Supermarkets.
* Restaurants.
* Cafes.
* Salons.
* Gyms.
* Workshops.
* Shopping centres.
* Hotels.
* Banks.
* Offices.
* Cinemas.

Public

* Schools.
* Universities.
* Hospitals.
* Clinics.
* Libraries.
* Museums.
* Theatres.
* Government buildings.
* Police stations.
* Fire stations.
* Courts.
* Community centres.
* Sports facilities.

Industrial

* Factories.
* Warehouses.
* Power facilities.
* Distribution centres.
* Manufacturing plants.
* Logistics facilities.

Every building type requires:

* Dimensions.
* Floor count.
* Room schedule.
* Floor plan.
* Elevations.
* Sections.
* Facade.
* Roof.
* Materials.
* Windows.
* Doors.
* Furniture.
* Fixtures.
* Lighting.
* Entrances.
* Accessibility.
* Utility connections.
* Navigation nodes.

Interior requirements

An enterable house contains:

* Entrance hall.
* Living room.
* Kitchen.
* Bedroom.
* Bathroom.

An enterable shop contains:

* Entrance.
* Sales floor.
* Shelves connected to inventory.
* Counter.
* Stockroom.
* Staff area.

An enterable public facility contains:

* Reception.
* Waiting area.
* Two consulting rooms.
* Staff room.

Default construction dimensions:

* Residential ceiling: 2.4–3.0 m.
* Commercial ceiling: 2.7–4.5 m.
* Door width: minimum 0.9 m.
* Door height: 2.0–2.1 m.
* Stair riser: 17 cm.
* Stair tread: 28 cm.
* Residential storey: 3.0 m.
* Commercial storey: 3.6 m.

Accessibility must be implemented.
